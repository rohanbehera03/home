Marketing Plan for Apple Inc.

I wrote a comprehensive marketing plan for Apple Inc. where I showed how Apple has performed financially in the past 5 years, what their current marketing situation looks like, and the objectives and issues facing the company. I performed a detailed SWOT analysis, assigned weights to each category, and discussed strategic actions Apple should take to continue growing in a competitive market.

This analysis also outlines the budget needed to support the recommended strategy and the steps Apple can take to remain on track to meet its goals over time.
